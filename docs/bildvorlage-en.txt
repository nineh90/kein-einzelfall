A calm, atmospheric, high-end editorial photograph in 16:9 landscape format for a German victim-support association.

Theme: {THEMA}
Concrete image idea: {MOTIV}

Warm sand, beige, cream and natural tones, soft golden sunlight, gentle shadows and natural reflections. A soft beam of light falls diagonally into the frame from the top left corner, creating a warm, hopeful, quiet atmosphere.

The imagery is calm, adult, serious, emotional and premium – minimalist, natural and not staged.

Composition: the actual subject is small and placed at the edge of the frame, in the lower left third. Nothing stands in the right half of the image: the upper area and the entire right half stay empty – only a plain warm wall, a bit of tabletop and soft light with gentle shadows. About 55 % of the image is calm negative space reserved for text added later.

No text in the image. No headlines, no lettering, no logo, no graphic elements.

Natural materials such as wood, linen, paper or ceramics may be used subtly.

No bright colors, no colorful flyer look, no cluttered symbols, no hectic scene, no dramatic or sad faces and no depiction of violence. Emotional, but not kitschy, sensational or preachy.

The image should look as if it belongs to one consistent professional photo shoot together with all other images of the association: same light, same color grading, same shallow depth of field, shot on a full-frame camera with a 50mm lens.
